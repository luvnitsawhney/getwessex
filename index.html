The Address

A Landmark Address Reimagined.

The Wessex stands on one of Bournemouth's most storied plots — the site of the original Wessex Hotel, opened in 1891, where Edwardian society came to promenade, take the sea air, and be seen. Today, that same spirit of privileged coastal living has been reborn in two elegant contemporary buildings, designed by award-winning Inland Homes.

Your apartment sits within this landmark development, just a five-minute walk from seven miles of Blue Flag beaches, and moments from the clifftop gardens, restaurants, and boutiques that make West Cliff one of the south coast's most desirable postcodes.

This is not a flat near the beach. This is a life at the sea.

The Life

Seven Miles of Blue Flag Beach.

Bournemouth has more sunshine than almost anywhere else in the UK. On a summer morning you can walk to the beach in five minutes, take the cable lift down the cliffs to the promenade, and cycle east along the shore as the town wakes up around you.

At the weekend, the clifftop restaurants fill with people who made the right decision. WestBeach for seafood with a view. The Triangle for something more bohemian. Westover Road — Bournemouth's Bond Street — for a different kind of Saturday afternoon.

Meyrick Park, with its 18-hole golf course, spa, and symphony concerts, is a mile away. Dean Park Cricket Ground is closer still. And when you want the city, London Waterloo is under two hours by train.

This is coastal life without compromise. The beach when you want it. The city when you need it. And on every ordinary Tuesday — a home that reminds you why you chose this.
